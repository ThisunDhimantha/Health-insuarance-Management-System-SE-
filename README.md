# 🏥 Web-Based Health Insurance Management System

## 📌 Project Overview

The Web-Based Health Insurance Management System is a software solution designed to simplify and automate health insurance operations. The system allows customers, insurance staff, and administrators to efficiently manage policies, claims, payments, and reports through a centralized platform.

The main goal of this project is to improve accuracy, reduce manual work, and provide a user-friendly platform for managing insurance-related activities.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Automate health insurance management processes.
- Provide an efficient platform for policy and claim management.
- Manage customer details, payments, and transactions securely.
- Generate and analyze insurance-related reports.
- Improve communication between customers and insurance administrators.

---

## 🚀 Main Features

### 👤 Customer Management
- Register and manage customer profiles.
- View insurance policy details.
- Submit and track claims.

### 📄 Policy Management
- Create and manage insurance policies.
- Update policy information.
- View policy status and details.

### 🏥 Medical Claim Management
- Submit medical claims.
- Review claim details.
- Approve or reject claims.

### 💳 Payment Management
- Process insurance payments.
- Maintain payment history.
- Generate payment records.

### 📊 Reporting & System Administration

**CRUD Operations**

#### Create
- Generate reports for policies, claims, payments, and system activities.

#### Read
- View, search, filter, and analyze generated reports.

#### Update
- Modify report settings such as report type, filters, and date range.

#### Delete
- Remove outdated or unnecessary reports.
