## Slide 2: Problem Statement & Vision
- **Problem:** Paper-based insurance management causes delays in claim processing, premium payment tracking errors, and high operational overhead.
- **Solution:** A centralized web platform automating insurance policies, claims, payments, and hospital verification.

---

## Slide 3: Main Modules & Core Features
1. **Policy Management:** Browse, compare, and manage policies.
2. **Medical Claim Management:** Digital claim filing and verification workflow.
3. **Premium Payments:** Online payment gateway integration.
4. **Hospital & Support Integration:** Verification portal and customer support ticket system.

---

- **Backend:** Java 17, Spring Boot 3.x, Spring Data JPA
- **Database:** MySQL 8.x
